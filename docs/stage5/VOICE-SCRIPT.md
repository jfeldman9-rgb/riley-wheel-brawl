# Stage 5 voice script

Teen/adult. Riley is sixteen and has already walked the Two Rivers, Baerlon, Caemlyn, and Shadar Logoth. Say the lines straight. No kid-show bounce, and no soft whisper unless the note asks for it.

Riley is ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, voice DYLO (re-recorded 2026-10-09, approved by Jason F via Grok Bot; prompts and hashes in `assets/audio/riley-voice-manifest-stage5.json`). Loial uses the Stage 4 Kokoro cast. The narrator, Aginor, Balthamel, and the Green Man are the ElevenLabs set. Spoken text below is the exact caption string in `src/stage5-voice.js`.

## Music

The fight uses one loop per track. Replace the silent stand-ins in place. Do not rename them. Loop points are the ones on `MUSIC` in `src/audio.js`.

### music-stage5

- **Title:** The Blight
- **File:** `assets/audio/music-stage5.mp3`
- **Id:** `stage5`
- **Loop:** 0.25 s to 40.25 s, gain 1
- **Brief:** Low brass drone, war drums, and distorted strings. Open and hostile, under the red sky of the Blight. Not a march a player could whistle. Teen/adult dread, not a cartoon stinger. Keep the drum bed even so the loop point does not land on a hit.

### music-boss5

- **Title:** The Eye
- **File:** `assets/audio/music-boss5.mp3`
- **Id:** `boss5`
- **Loop:** 0.25 s to 32 s, gain 0.94
- **Brief:** A choir swell for the Eye, plus a heartbeat pulse. The game does not swap tracks when Aginor hits phase 3, so write the faster pulse into the second half of this same loop. The choir is the Eye. The pulse is Aginor taking it. Still one seamless loop, loud enough to sit under the fight at gain 0.94.

## Turning a line on

Drop the mp3 at the path above and add its id to `present` in `assets/audio/stage5-voice-manifest.json`. `src/stage5-voice.js` reads that list into `VOICE_FILES`. Preload, `say()`, and the tether loop then play that file. An id left out of `present` stays a caption, including story lines, which call `say()` directly. Those ids are not requested, so a missing file does not 404. `tests/stage5-audio.test.mjs` fails if `present` and the files on disk disagree.

The plan for every line is `tools/stage5/audio-manifest.json`. The music plan is `tools/stage5/music-manifest.json`.
